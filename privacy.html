Sleep Cat Privacy Policy

Effective November 18, 2026

Sleep Cat picks a cat photo each morning to match how you slept. It is built to keep your data on your own devices.

Health data

With your permission, Sleep Cat reads sleep, heart rate, heart-rate variability, breathing rate, blood oxygen and wrist temperature from Apple Health. It uses this only on your iPhone and Apple Watch to score your night and choose a cat. Sleep Cat never writes to Apple Health, never sends Health data off your devices, and never shares it with anyone, including the developer. Health data is not used for advertising or marketing.

Photos

If you turn on the watch-face album, Sleep Cat adds each day's cat to a "Sleep Cat" album in your photo library. It does not read or upload your other photos.

Network use

The only thing Sleep Cat downloads is cat photos, from Wikimedia Commons. Those requests contain the address of the photo and nothing about you or your sleep. Wikimedia's own privacy policy applies to those requests.

What we don't do

Sleep Cat has no accounts, no analytics, no ads and no tracking. The developer does not collect, store or sell any personal information.

Deleting your data

Everything Sleep Cat stores lives on your devices. Deleting the app removes it. You can revoke Health or Photos access at any time in the Settings app.
